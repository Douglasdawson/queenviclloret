# PRODUCT.md — Queen Vic Sports Bar

register: brand

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Queen Vic, the original sports bar in Lloret de Mar (Costa Brava) since 1986. The site exists to put people on the terrace: show what's on the giant screen tonight, capture group and match-day reservations, and rank for "where to watch X in Lloret" searches (classic + AI engines). Five languages (EN default, ES, CA, FR, NL). Server-rendered for SEO; the design must never break SSR.

## Users

- **The deciding tourist** (primary): British or Irish, 25–55, on holiday in Lloret. On a phone, in sunlight by the pool or walking the strip at dusk, deciding in under a minute where to watch tonight's match. Wants instant proof: which game, what time, how big the screen, how good the craic.
- **The group organiser**: planning a stag/hen do or a lads' trip from the UK weeks ahead. Desktop or phone, comparing venues. Needs confidence (40 years, 1,250 m² terrace, photos of packed crowds) and a frictionless reservation request.
- **Locals and Spanish/Catalan visitors**: F1, MotoGP, big football nights. Less beer-tourism, same energy.

## Brand

"The Original since 1986." Queen Vic is a 40-year institution: a British pub heart beating on a Mediterranean terrace. The brand's physical reality (use it, don't invent): retro script logo "Queen Vic Terrace Bar" in charcoal with an offset gold shadow; bottle-green signage with gold script; a Queen Victoria postage-stamp emblem (gold crown, perforated edge, "Est. 1986"); a gold laurel "40 · EST. 1986" seal; festoon string lights, white sail shades and bunting over a packed terrace at golden hour.

Tone: cheeky pub confidence. Warm, direct, a little irreverent (the Queen Victoria stamp is a wink, not a museum piece). Never corporate, never shouty. The photos of real crowds do the bragging.

## Strategic Principles

1. **The real photo wins.** Golden-hour terrace shots are the hero of every key moment; design frames them, never competes with them.
2. **Heritage is the differentiator.** Every venue in Lloret has TVs; only Queen Vic has 40 years and the stamp. "The Original" is the claim everything hangs on.
3. **Fixtures are the product.** What's on tonight must read like a match programme: scannable in seconds on a phone in the sun.
4. **One action per page.** See what's on → come down / book the table. No competing CTAs.

## Anti-references

- Generic neon "SPORTS BAR" sites: black + electric blue, Bebas Neue everywhere, lightning bolts.
- Elementor/template look: identical card grids, stock DJ photos, counters animating from zero.
- Corporate restaurant-group polish that smells of marketing department.
- British-themed kitsch overload (Union Jack wallpaper). One wink (the stamp) is enough.
